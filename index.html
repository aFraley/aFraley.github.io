What I Build

Senior full-stack engineer and architect with 9 years building production systems across the stack: Python/Django and FastAPI on the backend, Vue and React on the frontend, PostgreSQL/PostGIS for data, and AWS for infrastructure. Geospatial and drone-based platforms are where I have gone deepest, turning raw sensor and imagery data into survey-grade deliverables.

Backend & APIs

Django and Django REST Framework, plus FastAPI, backed by PostgreSQL. Schema design and query optimization on multi-million-row tables, REST API design, background processing with Celery, and production transactional email infrastructure on Amazon SES with SNS/CloudWatch reputation monitoring.

Frontend & Interfaces

Vue and React applications, from CRUD product surfaces to data-heavy interactive tooling. Comfortable with Canvas and WebGL when the UI gets demanding, and focused on interfaces that non-engineers can actually use.

Cloud & Infrastructure

AWS across EKS, Lambda, and the serverless ecosystem, with Docker, Kubernetes, and CI/CD pipelines. Architecture decisions made to hold up under real customer load, plus MQTT-based IoT for real-time command, control, and telemetry.

Data & ETL

ETL pipelines and data at scale: PostgreSQL/PostGIS schema and index design, GeoPandas and GDAL for transforms, and Prefect- and Celery-orchestrated ingestion from raw input through processing to a QC'd result, with MLflow for experiment and model tracking. Transferable data-engineering fundamentals whatever the domain.

Geospatial & 3D

My deepest specialty, and an uncommon one. At Alynix I was a lead full-stack developer and architect on Decker, a mission-planning platform for bridge inspection: users plan whole corridors of bridges, upload the data once flown, and get a report that is downloadable and viewable live in the app, with contours rendered over a cloud-optimized GeoTIFF. A map interface shows where each plan sits and drives the reports the same way. The broader work spans photogrammetry pipelines (PDAL, GDAL, ODM), point clouds, and interactive Mapbox GL and three.js interfaces, the kind of background that is rare when a role calls for spatial or infrastructure data.
