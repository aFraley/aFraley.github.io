What I Build

Senior engineer with 9 years building and operating production Python systems on AWS — cloud infrastructure, Django/DRF backends, PostgreSQL/PostGIS data modeling, and data pipelines. Equally at home owning EKS/RDS production infrastructure or designing the APIs and schemas behind customer-facing products. Geospatial and drone-based platforms are where I have gone deepest, turning raw sensor and imagery data into survey-grade deliverables.

Backend & APIs

Django and Django REST Framework are the core of my work, backed by PostgreSQL/PostGIS. Multi-tenant architecture with organization-scoped access control, schema design and query optimization on multi-million-row tables, REST API design, background processing with Celery, and production transactional email on Amazon SES with SNS/CloudWatch reputation monitoring. FastAPI, Java/Spring Boot, and Go when a service calls for it.

Geospatial & 3D

My deepest specialty, and an uncommon one. At Alynix I was a lead developer and architect on Decker, a mission-planning platform for bridge inspection: users plan whole corridors of bridges, upload the data once flown, and get a report that is downloadable and viewable live in the app, with contours rendered over a cloud-optimized GeoTIFF. A map interface shows where each plan sits and drives the reports the same way. The broader work spans PostGIS raster and geospatial processing, photogrammetry pipelines (PDAL, GDAL, ODM, GeoPandas), point clouds, and interactive Mapbox GL and three.js interfaces — the kind of background that is rare when a role calls for spatial or infrastructure data.

Cloud & Infrastructure

Owned production AWS across EKS, EC2, RDS, Lambda and the serverless stack, Route 53, S3, SES, Secrets Manager/KMS, and CloudWatch — provisioned as code with Terraform and Ansible, containerized with Docker, and orchestrated on Kubernetes with CI/CD. Led a cost-and-consolidation initiative onto a shared multi-tenant Postgres RDS and executed near-zero-downtime database migrations of multi-GB PostGIS data, with documented runbooks and rollback snapshots. Plus MQTT-based IoT for real-time command, control, and telemetry.

Data & ETL

ETL and data-ingestion pipelines at scale: PostgreSQL/PostGIS schema and index design, GeoPandas and GDAL for transforms, and Prefect- and Celery-orchestrated ingestion from raw input through processing to a QC'd result, with MLflow for experiment and model tracking. Transferable data-engineering fundamentals whatever the domain.

Frontend & Interfaces

Vue 3 and React round out the product surface on top of the backend — from CRUD to data-heavy interactive tooling, including Mapbox GL and three.js for spatial and 3D views. Focused on interfaces that non-engineers can actually use.
