Geospatial Engineering

I build end-to-end platforms that turn raw drone, sensor, and imagery data into survey-grade deliverables — from interactive map-based editing UIs to PostGIS-backed APIs and cloud photogrammetry pipelines.

Interactive 2D Editing & QC

Mapbox GL + Canvas tooling for drawing flight plans, defining survey extents, and reviewing ML-detected polygons over orthomosaic backdrops. Snapping, multi-polygon operations, and coordinate transforms between pixel, image, and world frames.

Spatial Data Layer

PostGIS schemas and spatial indexes tuned for multi-million-row thermal image tables, deck geometry, and corridor polygons. R-tree, kd-tree, and quadtree lookups, coordinate system transforms, and geometry-heavy query optimization.

Cloud Photogrammetry Pipelines

PDAL and ODM pipelines producing orthomosaics (COG), classified point clouds (LAZ/COPC), and textured 3D meshes (glTF/GLB). Celery-orchestrated ingestion from raw capture through ML tagging to QC'd deliverable on Kubernetes/EKS.

3D Rendering & Mesh/Point-Cloud Editing

Browser-based three.js viewers for inspecting and editing textured meshes (glTF/GLB) and classified point clouds (LAZ/COPC) alongside their 2D counterparts. Selection, measurement, and classification tooling over streamed tiles, with camera, coordinate, and picking logic shared across 2D/3D views. Prototyping Gaussian splatting as an additional deliverable format.
